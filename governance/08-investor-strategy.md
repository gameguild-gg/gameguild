# 08 — Investor Strategy

## Instruments (by stage)

| Stage | Instrument | Investor gets |
|---|---|---|
| Angels | SAFE (equity) + token warrant | equity + right to governance tokens later |
| Pre-seed/Seed | SAFE or priced round + token side-letter | equity + token allocation w/ vesting |
| Future | token round (SAFT) if ever external token sale | tokens only |

Default: **equity-first, tokens via warrant/side-letter** — safest legally, investors understand it, tokens stay internal.

## Investor token terms

- Vesting: 1–2y, 6–12m cliff
- Lockup: no transfer while tokens non-transferable (default state)
- Governance: full voting rights; consider vote-weight cap if any single investor > 20% of supply
- Profit share: same as everyone, pro-rata from entry snapshot

## Reserve math (from 04)

Investor pool 15–25% of total, released in tranches per round; unraised reserve returns to founders' unvested balance or treasury by vote — decide at incorporation.

## Dilution hygiene

- Anti-dilution / pro-rata: standard legal docs, not on-chain
- Founder vesting protects against dead equity
- Keep option pool sized at raise time (10–15%), not inflated early
