# 02 — Problem and Vision

## Problem

Traditional company structures make three things hard for a small, distributed, crypto-native founding team:

1. **Governance rigidity** — amending shareholder agreements is slow and lawyer-heavy; founders in two countries (BR/US) multiply the friction.
2. **Profit distribution overhead** — cross-border dividends are slow, expensive, and tax-opaque.
3. **Investor alignment** — angels/seed want upside; a transparent revenue-share structure can offer both equity and clear quarterly payout rights.

## Vision

A company where:

- Product direction and treasury spending are decided by transparent, equity-weighted votes.
- Every revenue stream flows through auditable on-chain infrastructure (treasury multisig + splitter contracts).
- Every 3 years, holders vote the profit-share mandate: how much of accumulated earnings distributes, how much reinvests, and in what form (lump sum, streaming, buyback).
- Founders, team, and investors hold the same instrument class, aligned by the same rules.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Public token sale / TGE — not planned at this stage.
- On-chain governance — decisions run through the operating agreement + Loomio; the legal wrapper protects, the process governs.
