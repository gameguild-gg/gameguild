# 02 — Problem and Vision

## Problem

Traditional company structures make three things hard for a small, distributed, crypto-native founding team:

1. **Governance rigidity** — amending shareholder agreements is slow and lawyer-heavy; governors in two countries (BR/US) multiply the friction.
2. **Profit distribution overhead** — cross-border dividends are slow, expensive, and tax-opaque.
3. **Investor alignment** — angels/seed want upside; a transparent revenue-share structure can offer both equity and clear quarterly payout rights.

## Vision

A company where:

- Product direction and treasury spending are decided by transparent, equity-weighted votes with qualified majorities that no two-holder coalition can capture.
- Every quarter, governors vote the profit-share mandate: how much of the quarter's earnings distributes to revenue shareholders, how much reinvests.
- Work and ownership are separated: whoever works does so under contract; whoever owns receives voted distributions.
- Revenue from the marketplace flows creator-first: sale proceeds primarily to the content creator, a small platform fee to the company.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Public token sale / TGE — not planned at this stage.
- On-chain governance — decisions run through the operating agreement + Loomio; the legal wrapper protects, the process governs.
