# 01 — Executive Summary

> Status: DRAFT. Numbers and structure are working assumptions pending founder agreement and legal review.

## One-liner

GameGuild runs a DAO-style decision structure — founder voting on product and treasury, profit-share mandates re-decided every 3 years, crypto payouts — implemented through a traditional legal wrapper with **off-chain voting and an operating agreement, no tokens**.

## Core structure

- **Founders**: Tolsta 40% / Matheus 30% / Joel 30% of founder shares (vesting TBD — must be decided before anything is issued)
- **Governance scope**: product decisions + treasury usage; equity-weighted votes, off-chain
- **Profit sharing**: distribution model re-decided by vote every 3 years
- **Payout rails**: stablecoin (USDC) — crypto payouts survive the DAO drop; only token mechanics are gone
- **Structure**: Delaware DevCo (Tolsta US-resident: equity + comp) + Cayman Foundation **optional/deferred** until real treasury scale; BR service entity if BR-resident holders
- **Investor reserve**: carved from a dedicated pool, NOT from founder percentages retroactively

## What changed from the DAO plan

Tokens, Governor/Timelock, Snapshot, on-chain splitters — all deferred. The *decision architecture* (what we vote on, thresholds, the 3-year mandate) is unchanged and is now enforced by contract (operating/shareholder agreement) instead of smart contracts. If a DAO layer ever returns, this structure maps 1:1 onto it.
