# Security Policy

> **Found a vulnerability?** Please report it privately — don't open a public issue. We take every report seriously.

---

## How to report

1. **Send a private message** to a maintainer on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/9CdJeQ2XKB), or use [gameguild.gg/contact](https://gameguild.gg/contact)
2. **Include as much detail as you can:**

| What to include | Why it helps |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Description of the vulnerability | So we understand the issue |
| Steps to reproduce | So we can verify and fix it |
| Affected components or files | So we know where to look |
| Potential impact | So we can prioritize correctly |

> Don't worry if you can't fill everything — any amount of detail helps.

---

## What happens next

GameGuild is a small, community-driven project maintained by volunteers. We don't have a dedicated security team or budget, so our timelines reflect that reality — but we take every report seriously.

| Step | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| We acknowledge your report | Within **7 days** |
| We complete an initial assessment | Within **14 days** |
| We work on a fix | **30–90 days**, depending on severity and complexity |

We'll keep you updated along the way and let you know when the fix lands in `main`.

---

## Coordinated disclosure

We ask that you wait **90 days after the fix reaches `main`** before publishing details publicly. This gives the community time to update.

After that window, you're free — and encouraged — to disclose. We'll also publish an advisory crediting your contribution.

> If 90 days feels unreasonable for a specific case, let's talk. We're flexible.

---

## What's in scope

This policy covers the code in **this repository**. Third-party dependencies, hosting infrastructure, and external services are outside our control — but we still appreciate hearing about issues in those areas.

---

## Recognition

We credit every reporter in our security advisories (unless you prefer to stay anonymous). We don't currently offer monetary bounties, but valid reports are acknowledged in the project's **changelog and documentation**.

Thank you for helping keep GameGuild safe for everyone.
