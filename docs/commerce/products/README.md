> Scope: the product catalog and monetization surface — browse/detail/search, catalog administration (create/update/batch/lifecycle, categories, bundles, inventory), pricing display (versioned `ProductPricing`, pricing rules, tiers, A/B pricing experiments), the promo engine (codes, campaigns, stacking, usage, attribution, CAPTCHA/anomaly defenses), user-product entitlements, and the in-app support-ticket system the module also hosts. - Mermaid labels contain no parentheses and no colons (repo rendering constraint); route custom actions are spelled `id-colon-action`.

## Module shape at a glance

- **Controllers (11, ~92 endpoints)**: `ProductsController` (`v{v}/products`, 15), `PromoCodesController` (15), `SupportTicketsController` (12), `PromoCampaignsController` (11), `PricingRulesController` (9), `PricingExperimentsController` (9), `EntitlementsController` (`v{v}/entitlements`, 5), `ProductCategoriesController` (5), `PromoStackingRulesController` (5), `MySupportTicketsController` (4), `UserEntitlementsController` (2).
- **Entities (25 files)**: `Product` (+ `ProductCommissionConfig`, `ProductBundleItem`, `ProductInventory`), `ProductCategory` (hierarchical, tenant-unique slug), `ProductPricing` + `ProductPricingVersion` (immutable price history), `PricingTier`, `ProductSubscriptionPlan` (legacy orphan — see `06-gaps.md` P5), `UserProduct` (access rows), `PromoCode`/`PromoCampaign`/`PromoCodeUse`/`PromoCodeUserUsageCounter`/`PromoCodeValidationAttempt`/`PromoCampaignAttribution`/`PromoStackingRule`/`PromoValidityWindow`/`PromoCustomerType`, `SupportTicket`(+Message); pricing rules & experiments live in `Commerce:/Entities/` and are configured by the Products module (`Products:/ProductsModule.cs:268-349`).
- **Services**: `PricingEngineService` (effective price → rule discount → experiment arm → promo codes, `Products:/Services/PricingEngineService.cs:10-120`), `PromoCodeService` + gates (`PromoRedemptionCaptchaGate`, `PromoUsageAnomalyEvaluator`, `PromoCustomerStateResolver`), `EntitlementService`, `UserProductService`, `ProductCategoryValidator`, `BundleSavingsCalculator`, `PricingExperimentService` (+ sticky `PricingExperimentAllocator`), plus the two cross-module coordinators `SubscriptionPromoCoordinator` and `FraudReviewSupportTicketCoordinator`.
- **Background service (1)**: `PricingSchedulingBackgroundService` (issue #395 scheduling sweep — **never hosted**: P1).
- **Wiring reality** (the package's central finding): handlers/validators register via module scan (`API:/Core/Setup/ApplicationLayerExtensions.cs:110-145`, `Commerce.Products` in `ModuleConfiguration.CommonEnabledModules:20`); repositories and `I{Name}Service` register by the host's convention scanner (`API:/Core/Setup/InfrastructureLayerExtensions.cs:482-560`); six non-convention services are registered explicitly at `:275-290`. Everything else that only `AddProductsModule` registers (`ProductsModule.cs:16-66`) — the hosted scheduler, the four options classes, and five promo-engine services — is **absent from the composed host**. See `02-cqrs-wiring-matrix.md` §0 and `06-gaps.md` P1-P4.

## Headline findings

1. **`AddProductsModule` has no host caller** (`ProductsModule.cs:16` — only the unit test calls it): the pricing scheduler never runs, `PricingSchedulingOptions`/`PromoEngineOptions`/`PromoAbuseDetectionOptions`/`PromoRedemptionCaptchaOptions` bind to nothing (not even the module binds sections — `AddOptions<T>()` without `.Bind` at `:53-60`), and five promo services are unregistered (`06-gaps.md` P1).
2. **Subscription creation with promo codes fails hard** — `CreateSubscriptionCommandHandler` throws when `ISubscriptionPromoCoordinator` is null and promo codes were supplied (`Subs:/Commands/CreateSubscription/CreateSubscriptionCommandHandler.cs:83-87`); the coordinator implementation exists but is only registered by the never-called module method (`ProductsModule.cs:36`). Fail-closed, as an unplanned 500 (`06-gaps.md` P2).
3. **Fraud-review tickets silently skip** — `ProcessPaymentCommandHandler` and `SubscriptionChangeFraudGate` take `IFraudReviewTicketCoordinator? = null` (`Payments:/Commands/ProcessPayment/ProcessPaymentCommandHandler.cs:27`, `Subs:/Commands/SubscriptionChangeFraudGate.cs:66`); with the coordinator unregistered, issue #409's "reviews surface in the support queue" behavior quietly never happens (`06-gaps.md` P3).
4. **Anti-farming/anti-abuse defenses are off** — the CAPTCHA gate, the usage-anomaly evaluator and the customer-state resolver are optional constructor params that resolve to null in the host, so anonymous promo probing is ungated and customer-type-restricted codes fail closed as `CustomerNotEligible` (`Products:/Services/PricingEngineService.cs:312-318`, `06-gaps.md` P4).
5. **Price source of truth post-#1266 is the plan, not the product** — subscription billing amounts derive from `SubscriptionPlan.MonthlyPriceInCents` via `SubscriptionPlanPricingResolver` (`Subs:/Services/SubscriptionPlanPricingResolver.cs:30-62`) and the payment handler rejects any mismatch (`Payments:/Commands/ProcessPayment/ProcessPaymentCommandHandler.cs:50-53`); `ProductPricing` is catalog/display pricing only, and `ProductSubscriptionPlan` is a legacy orphan table nothing reads. Consistency between what the catalog displays and what is billed is a UI discipline, not an enforced invariant (`05-cross-module-sequences.md` §5, `06-gaps.md` P5/P6).
