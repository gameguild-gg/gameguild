# Legal and Dispute Process

This document describes how to report and handle legal concerns related to this Project.

## Contact

Legal notices should be sent to:

**Contact:** [https://gameguild.gg/contact](https://gameguild.gg/contact)

---

## Notice Requirements

If you believe that any part of this Project infringes your intellectual property rights (including copyright or patents), please provide:

* identification of the affected material (file, feature, or component)
* description of the claimed rights
* explanation of the alleged infringement
* your contact information
* a statement that the information provided is accurate and made in good faith

Incomplete notices may delay processing.

---

## Review Process

Upon receiving a valid notice, the Maintainer will:

* acknowledge receipt within a reasonable time
* review the claim in good faith
* request additional information if necessary
* take appropriate action, which may include temporarily restricting, removing, or replacing the affected material

---

## Resolution

The Project prioritizes:

* clarification of the issue
* correction of the material
* removal or replacement when necessary

Where applicable, the Maintainer may contact the Contributor responsible for the material.

Contribution records (such as version control history) may be used to identify the origin of the material.

---

## Counter-Notice

If a Contributor believes that a claim is incorrect, they may respond with relevant information supporting their position.

The Maintainer may consider both sides in good faith before determining further action.

---

## Good Faith Resolution

Before initiating any formal legal action, parties are encouraged to contact the Maintainer through the process described in this document.

The Project is committed to reviewing and addressing valid concerns promptly and in good faith.

Failure to follow this process may limit the Project’s ability to respond or resolve the issue efficiently.

This process is intended to facilitate resolution without unnecessary escalation and may be considered as evidence of good faith efforts by the Project.
