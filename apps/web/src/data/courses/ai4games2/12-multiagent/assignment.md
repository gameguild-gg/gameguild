# Final Project — Checkpoint 3: Proof of Concept

**Due:** Sunday, 2026/04/05

## Overview

This week your team must demonstrate that the **core AI algorithm is working**. The goal is to show tangible progress — not a polished game, but clear evidence that the central AI mechanic functions.

## Deliverables

Provide **one** of the following:

1. **Playable Build** — Publish a WebGL build to [itch.io](https://itch.io/) or [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) where the AI behavior can be observed. Even a minimal scene with placeholder art is fine as long as the AI runs.

2. **Video Demo** — Record a 2–5 minute video showing the AI in action. Narrate what is happening and point out the AI-driven behavior. Upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine) or share via Google Drive.

## What "Core Algorithm Working" Means

- The fundamental AI technique (e.g., pathfinding, planning, decision-making) produces correct output.
- It runs in real-time within the game context (not just a standalone test).
- You can describe what it does, where it succeeds, and where it still needs work.

::: tip

Do not worry about polish, art, or edge cases yet. Focus on getting the AI loop running: **sense → decide → act**.

:::
