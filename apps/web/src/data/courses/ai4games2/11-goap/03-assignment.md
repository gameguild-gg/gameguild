## Overview

This week you will design and document the architecture of your AI system. The deliverable is a **system diagram** showing every major component, data flow, and how the AI integrates with the game.

## Deliverables

Submit a document or diagram that includes:

1. **System Diagram** — A visual representation of your AI architecture. Show modules, their responsibilities, and how they communicate. Recommended tools:
   - [Mermaid](https://mermaid.live/)
   - [draw.io](https://draw.io/)
   - [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com/)

2. **Component Breakdown** — For each major component, write 2–3 sentences describing its purpose, inputs, and outputs.

3. **Data Flow** — Show how game state flows into the AI system and how AI decisions flow back into the game. Include any data structures or message formats you plan to use.

4. **Feasibility Notes** — Identify the riskiest part of your implementation and describe how you plan to address it. What will you prototype first?

::: tip

If you are unsure about your architecture, look at the case studies from class (F.E.A.R. GOAP, Killzone blackboard, Halo 2 behavior trees) for structural inspiration.

:::
