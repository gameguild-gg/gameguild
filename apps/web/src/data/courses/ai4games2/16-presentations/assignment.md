# Final Project — Checkpoint 7: Final Presentations

**Presentations:** Monday 2026/04/27 (Group A) & Thursday 2026/04/30 (Group B)

## Overview

This is the culmination of your final project. Each team will deliver a **10-minute presentation** followed by a **5-minute Q&A**. A **live demo** of your AI system is required during the presentation.

## Presentation Guidelines

- **Duration:** 10 minutes (strict) + 5 minutes Q&A.
- **Live Demo:** You must show your AI running live. Have a backup video in case of technical issues.
- **Audience:** Your classmates — target the content level appropriately.
- **Goal:** Teach the class your chosen topic. Explain the algorithm, show it running, and discuss what you learned.
- **Tools:** Any presentation tool works. [Google Slides](https://www.google.com/slides/) recommended for easy sharing.

::: tip

Practice your presentation at least once with your team. Time yourself. The 10-minute limit will be enforced.

:::

## Suggested Presentation Structure

1. **Introduction** (1 min) — What is the topic? Why is it interesting for games?
2. **Algorithm Explanation** (3 min) — How does the technique work? Use diagrams.
3. **Live Demo** (3 min) — Show the AI in action. Point out the key behaviors.
4. **Challenges & Lessons** (2 min) — What was hard? What would you do differently?
5. **Conclusion** (1 min) — Summary and takeaways.
