## Overview

This week is **in-class playtesting**. You will bring your current build and have classmates test your AI system. You will also test other teams' projects and provide feedback.

## What to Prepare

- A **playable WebGL build** deployed and accessible (itch.io or GitHub Pages).
- A short (1–2 sentence) description of what testers should look for or try to do.
- The **feedback form** ready to collect responses (a shared Google Form or spreadsheet works well).

## During Class

1. **Play other teams' builds** — Spend ~5 minutes per project. Focus on the AI behavior: Does it work? Is it interesting? Is it broken?
2. **Collect feedback on your project** — Have testers fill out your feedback form.
3. **Take notes** — Write down bugs, confusing moments, and suggestions you receive.

## Deliverables

By the end of the week, each team should have:

1. A playable build that classmates tested.
2. Collected feedback (forms/notes).
3. A short list of **top 3 issues** identified from testing, prioritized for next week.
