# Week 01 Readings — Network Fundamentals

**Tuesday Topics:** OSI model, TCP/IP stack  
**Friday Topics:** Network devices, packets, basic addressing

## Required Readings

| #   | Reading                                                                                                                                                                 | Time   | Covers                                                 |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Glenn Fiedler, ["What Every Programmer Needs to Know About Game Networking"](https://gafferongames.com/post/what_every_programmer_needs_to_know_about_game_networking/) | 10 min | Why networking matters for games (motivational opener) |
| 2   | Beej's Guide to Network Concepts, [Ch. 4 "The Layered Network Model"](https://beej.us/guide/bgnet0/html/split/the-layered-network-model.html)                           | 15 min | OSI vs TCP/IP, encapsulation, layer responsibilities   |
| 3   | Practical Networking, ["OSI Model"](https://www.practicalnetworking.net/series/packet-traveling/osi-model/)                                                             | 15 min | Animated GIFs showing packets through layers           |
| 4   | Kurose & Ross, [Chapter 1: Introduction](https://gaia.cs.umass.edu/kurose_ross/ppt-9e/Chapter_1_v9.0.pptx) (PowerPoint slides)                                          | 30 min | Computer networks and the Internet, protocol layers    |

**Total reading time: ~70 minutes**
