## Overview

This week you will form your team and submit a project proposal. The proposal must be in **slideshow format** (Google Slides, PowerPoint, etc.) and shared with the instructor.

## Deliverables

Submit a slideshow containing:

1. **Team Members & Roles** — List every member and their primary responsibility (e.g., network protocol, client, server, testing).
2. **Chosen Topic** — Pick from the [topic suggestions table](../09-break/finalproject.md) or propose your own (must be approved by the instructor first).
3. **Scope Description** — Describe what your networked application will do, what problem it solves, and what "done" looks like. Be specific enough that someone outside your team can understand the goal.
4. **Tech Stack** — Which language, libraries, and tools you will use. Specify transport protocol (TCP, UDP, or both) and any frameworks (Boost.Asio, WebSockets, etc.).
5. **Protocol Sketch** — A preliminary diagram or description of the messages your application will exchange. This does not need to be final — just show you have thought about the wire format.
6. **Timeline** — A rough week-by-week plan from now through Week 16.

::: tip

Keep the slides concise — aim for 5–8 slides. You will present next week in class.

:::
