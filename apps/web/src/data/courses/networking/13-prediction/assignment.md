## Overview

This week is **in-class testing**. You will bring your current build and have classmates test your networked application. You will also test other teams' projects and provide feedback.

## What to Prepare

- A **testable build** that others can run (GitHub repo with clear instructions, Docker Compose, or hosted demo).
- A short (1–2 sentence) description of what testers should try to do and what to look for.
- The **feedback form** ready to collect responses (a shared Google Form or spreadsheet works well).
- Instructions for simulating network conditions if applicable (e.g., how to test with artificial latency using `tc` or Clumsy).

## During Class

1. **Test other teams' builds** — Spend ~5 minutes per project. Focus on the networking behavior: Does it connect? Is it responsive? Does it handle latency gracefully? Does it break under stress?
2. **Collect feedback on your project** — Have testers fill out your feedback form.
3. **Take notes** — Write down bugs, disconnections, confusing moments, and suggestions you receive.

## Deliverables

By the end of the week, each team should have:

1. A testable build that classmates tested.
2. Collected feedback (forms/notes).
3. A short list of **top 3 issues** identified from testing, prioritized for next week.
