## Objective:

In this assignment, you will develop a concept for a new game, express your ideas using various tools, and present your game idea to the class. This will help you understand the game design process, from brainstorming to presentation.

## Overview:

Game Idea Brainstorming:

You will start by brainstorming game concepts, considering the following:

- Game genre (e.g., action, puzzle, RPG)
- Target audience
- Unique gameplay mechanics
- Storyline or theme
- Art style

Tools to Express Your Game Idea: Use the following tools to develop and express your game concept:

- Miro (for brainstorming and mind maps)
- Figma (for wireframes and UI mockups)
- Trello or Notion (for organizing tasks, features, and timelines)
- Concept Art: Use any drawing software (e.g., Photoshop, Krita, or even hand-drawn sketches scanned in) to create visual representations of your game.
- Game Design Document (GDD): Create a simple GDD using Google Docs or Microsoft Word to outline the core aspects of your game, such as: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1axeeBWp683LPU8gCBQQqmquHMYHuG3uhNTN0LjSJBKk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.ptaao3gc583z or notion: https://www.notion.so/templates/game-design-document
- Story synopsis
- Gameplay mechanics - Core and secondary mechanics
- Game levels or stages
- Character design
- Art style and mood board

## Presentation:

At the end of the week, you will present your game idea to the class using the following:

PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canvas, Notion or any other presentation tool (to show your concept art, wireframes, mind maps, and GDD)

A 5-minute pitch of your game idea, covering its main aspects:

- genre,
- core gameplay mechanic,
- storyline,
- target audience

Example of game pitches: https://heyglitch.notion.site/Pitch-Decks-f56e38c13fe6417f8379859e74367e1a

## Grading Criteria:

- Creativity (25%): How unique and interesting is your game concept?
- Clarity (25%): Is your idea clearly communicated through the use of the tools?
- Detail (25%): How well have you fleshed out your game idea in the GDD?
- Presentation (25%): Did you effectively present your game idea to the class?
