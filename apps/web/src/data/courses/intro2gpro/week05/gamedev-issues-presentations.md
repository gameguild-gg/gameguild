# Game development issues presentations

This week the students will be presenting their research about game development issues. Each student of the group will present their own research about a game development issue.

The presentations will be 10-15 minutes long. And the class will be able to ask questions and discuss the presented research.

In a online environment, this would be a peer evaluated presentation. The students should record a video about their research and upload to the system in order to get feedback from the class. Every student will be assigned to give feedback to at least 3 other students.
