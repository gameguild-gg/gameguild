## Overview

This week is **in-class peer testing**. You will demonstrate your current system and have classmates test it. You will also test other teams' projects and provide feedback.

## What to Prepare

- Your **Docker Compose stack running** on your laptop or a deployed URL.
- A short (1–2 sentence) description of what testers should try to do.
- A **feedback form** ready to collect responses (a shared Google Form or spreadsheet works well).
- A `README.md` in your repo with clear setup instructions.

## During Class

1. **Test other teams' systems** — Spend ~5 minutes per project. Focus on: Does `docker-compose up` work? Can you interact with the system? Are the databases being used as described?
2. **Collect feedback on your project** — Have testers fill out your feedback form.
3. **Take notes** — Write down bugs, confusing moments, and suggestions you receive.

## Deliverables

By the end of the week, each team should have:

1. A system that classmates tested (running via Docker Compose or deployed URL).
2. Collected feedback (forms/notes).
3. A short list of **top 3 issues** identified from testing, prioritized for next week.
