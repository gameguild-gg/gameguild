## Overview

This week you will form your team and submit a project proposal. The proposal must be in **slideshow format** (Google Slides, PowerPoint, etc.) and shared with the instructor.

## Deliverables

Submit a slideshow containing:

1. **Team Members & Roles** — List every member and their primary responsibility (e.g., backend lead, data pipeline, frontend, DevOps).
2. **Chosen Topic** — Pick from the [topic suggestions table](../09-break/final-project.md) or propose your own (must be approved by the instructor first).
3. **Database Selection** — Which 3+ databases you will use and **why** each one was chosen for its specific role. Justify your choices using the decision framework from Week 01.
4. **Scope Description** — Describe what your system will do and what "done" looks like. Be specific enough that someone outside your team can understand the goal.
5. **Timeline** — A rough week-by-week plan from now through Week 16.

::: tip

Keep the slides concise — aim for 5–8 slides. You will present briefly in class next week.

:::
