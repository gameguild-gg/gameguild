## Overview

This is the culmination of your final project. Each team will deliver a **10-minute presentation** followed by a **5-minute Q&A**. A **live demo** of your Docker Compose system is required during the presentation.

## Presentation Guidelines

- **Duration:** 10 minutes (strict) + 5 minutes Q&A.
- **Live Demo:** You must show your system running via `docker-compose up` live. Have a backup video in case of technical issues.
- **Audience:** Your classmates — target the content level appropriately.
- **Goal:** Teach the class about your chosen domain and database architecture. Explain why you chose each database, show the system running, and discuss what you learned.
- **Tools:** Any presentation tool works. [Google Slides](https://www.google.com/slides/) recommended for easy sharing and multi-user editing.

::: tip

Practice your presentation at least once with your team. Time yourself. The 10-minute limit will be enforced.

:::

## Suggested Presentation Structure

1. **Introduction** (1 min) — What problem does your system solve? Why is it interesting?
2. **Architecture** (3 min) — What databases did you use and why? Show your system diagram.
3. **Live Demo** (3 min) — Run `docker-compose up`, show the system working, demonstrate key features.
4. **Challenges & Lessons** (2 min) — What was hard? What would you do differently? What did you learn about polyglot persistence?
5. **Conclusion** (1 min) — Summary and takeaways.
